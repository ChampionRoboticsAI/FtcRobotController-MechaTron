# Inspection Checklist
FTC has an [inspection checklist](https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/archive/2027/event/inspection-check) for teams to follow for each meet. 

Each meet have 5 games and the team needs to have a pre and post game checklist to follow in a similar layout as above. 

Use [GameDayChecklist.xlsx](GameDayChecklist.xlsx) at each meet to track these checks per game (Game 1-5), with Status, Checked By and Comments columns, a Meet Summary, and a Lessons Learned log. Save a copy per meet. If you change items here, update the spreadsheet too.

## Pre Game Checklist
Make sure the team goes through this checklist 15 minutes before each game.

- All Mecannum wheels are tight and secured. We do not want our wheels falling off in middle of a game.
- All odometer pods are secure and JST cables are intact
- Flower arm mechanism is secure
- IMPORTANT: All hex shaft inner and outer collars are secure.
- Runnerbands on all intake rollers are intact.
- Rubberbands on side of flower hood are intact.
- Rubberbands on flower hood top flap are intact.
- Rubberband on catapult arms are intact and equal on both sides.
- Catapult mechanism is intact.
- All REV chains are intact and on the sprockets.
- The foam on crossbar for nectar intack to flower hood is intact.
- All electrical cables and connections are intact.
- Battery is fully charged and secured in the slot on back of the robot.
- Hopper area is clean and intact.
- Driver hub is fully charged and ready to go.
- Replace any loose or broken rubber bands. 

### Additional pre game checks (suggested, review after first meet)

- Battery voltage reads 13.0V or higher on telemetry/battery tester (record the reading).
- Main power switch is reachable and the robot powers on cleanly.
- Wires are zip-tied away from wheels, chains and catapult; no loose ends.
- Control Hub and Expansion Hub status LEDs are normal (no flashing errors).
- Driver Hub is connected to the correct Control Hub Wi-Fi; no other devices paired.
- Both gamepads are plugged in via USB and assigned (Start+A / Start+B).
- Correct robot configuration is active on the Robot Controller.
- Latest tested code is deployed (no untested changes since last game).
- Quick function test: drive all directions, intake, catapult, flower arm.
- Odometry/Pinpoint telemetry reads correctly and resets at the start pose.
- Correct Auto OpMode selected for alliance color and start position.
- Robot placed exactly on start position/orientation and preload loaded.
- Robot fits in the starting size limit with all mechanisms stowed.
- Correct alliance-color robot signs / team number displayed.
- Auto paths and endgame plan agreed with alliance partner.
- Drive team roles confirmed, safety glasses on, spare battery and tool kit at the queue.


## Post Game Checklist
Make sure the team goes through this checklist right after each game.

- Inspect for any broken 3D parts and replace before next game
- Inspect all electronics
- Remove battery and charge it
- Charge driver hub

### Additional post game checks (suggested, review after first meet)

- Record the score breakdown (Auto / TeleOp / Endgame / Penalties).
- 5-minute drive team debrief: what worked and what did not.
- Review penalties with the drive coach; ask the Head Referee right away if unclear.
- Re-check wheels, hex shaft collars and chains for loosening.
- Check all rubber bands (intake, hood, catapult) for wear and replace.
- Check for loose screws/bolts; feel motors for overheating.
- Clean wheels, intake and hopper of debris.
- If a disconnect or fault occurred, save the Robot Controller logs before restarting.
- Log any code/tuning changes needed (do not deploy untested code).
- Check next match number, time, alliance color and partner.
- Return tools, spare parts and game elements to the pit box.


## Handle with care

- The robot: it is heavy and any damage will set us back and we may not be able to compete. Carry it with two people.
- Driver Hub: make sure it's always charged and do not drop as it might loosen the battery and shut down the driver hub in middle of the game.
- Gamepad: make sure it is securely connected via a USB cable to the driver hub. Any loose connection or disconnect from driver hub will impact our game.
- Battery: Do not drop and always keep it fully charged. Never use a battery with damaged wires or connectors.
